About Our Project

Local Farmers Support Portal is a CSE 326 project. Its aim is to help small farmers connect with local customers and make their products easy to find.

Main Features: Farmer Profiles, Product Listings, Price, Quantity, Search, Location, Ratings and Product Requests.
